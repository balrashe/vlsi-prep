## Shared look (every output style)

- **Footage**: single talking head, mid shot, seated in a gaming chair, warm practical lamp left, posters on wall, cool-grey wall. Slight warm grade, lifted blacks, no visible vignette.
- **Caption (spoken words)**: white `#ffffff`, sentence case, semibold geometric sans (SF Pro Display / Inter family look), letter-spacing slightly tight. 1–3 words on screen at a time. Height of lowercase run 40–52 px (e.g. "at C tier" spans y 934–973). Centred horizontally. No box, no stroke, very soft shadow.
- **Accent word**: one word per phrase swaps to an italic serif in orange (`~#ce6f2b` to `#e08a3a`), same baseline, about 1.15x the sans size ("it an *example*", y 1430–1477).
- **Title (hook)**: two lines at the top. Line 1 white bold sans with soft shadow; line 2 black text inside a solid white box (`#fefefe`, no radius). Examples: "Make your videos / [too sticky to skip]" (line 1 y 215–292, about 78 px cap-to-descender), "How I got Claude / [to edit my reels.]" (y 107–179). Box hugs text with about 24 px side padding.
- **Orange accent colour**: `#e08a3a` family. Used for the ranking title, the accent serif word, the youtube-clips highlight.

## Motion and pacing (what can be measured)

- Hard cuts only between styles, every 2.8 s on the beat. No whip, zoom or dissolve between outputs.
- Captions change on every spoken word or short phrase (1–3 words); in the 10 fps strips, they appear with no fade.
- Loading reveal in the app (not part of the styles): dot-matrix of orange dots resolving into the frame over ~0.6 s.
- **Not measurable here**: B-roll durations, zoom punch-ins, sound effects (music covers everything), card drop easing, split timing.
