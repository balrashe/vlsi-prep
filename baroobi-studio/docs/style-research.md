## Style options

- **A. Talking head + floating proof cards.** baroobi.inc, volkan.js, digitalsamaritan, maverickgpt. Fastest; the 1.3M reel is this. Cost: lowest (existing /baroobi-reel format).
- **B. Green-screen / cutout over real UI.** baroobi.inc (DdXC, DcrN), wassimyounes_ opener. Product fills the frame. Cost: low.
- **C. Split screen with bespoke 2D motion graphics on top.** jackroberts___, alan.buildz, charlieautomates, chase.h.ai (cheap end). Biggest premium lift in the sample. Cost: medium; template the recurring cards.
- **D. Pixel-art mascot dioramas in a split panel.** opusjake. Mascot acts out each point. Cost: medium (art authoring), cheap to render.
- **E. Voxel 3D diorama + corner cutout.** wassimyounes_ only. Constant motion and depth. Cost: medium-high to author, cheap to render in Three.js.
- **F. Faceless avatar host over clean UI.** charlieautomates. No filming needed. Cost: low-medium.
- **G. Lifestyle b-roll montage.** steph.osmak. Pace benchmark only; needs filming.

Not seen anywhere in the sample: smooth product-render 3D, AI-generated video.
The pixel Claude crab already appears at opusjake, jackroberts___ and wassimyounes_.
